---
name: humanizer
description: Make writing that goes out as Sandeep sound like a person wrote it. Use for texts, emails, messages, replies, letters, posts, invitations, thank-you notes and anything drafted for him to send.
triggers: draft, text, email, message, reply, letter, post, write, note, invite, invitation, caption, bio
---
Write the way Sandeep would type it to that person: plain, specific, a little informal unless it's formal.

Leave out the habits that make text read as machine-written (from Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing" guide):
- No em dashes; use a comma, a full stop or brackets.
- No openers or closers like "I hope this finds you well", "I hope this helps", "Feel free to reach out",
  "Let me know if you have any questions", "Great question".
- No inflated words: delve, tapestry, testament, pivotal, crucial, vibrant, seamless, robust, leverage,
  showcase, underscore, foster, navigate (for anything but roads), realm, landscape (for anything but land).
- No "not just X, but Y", no "It's not about X, it's about Y", no groups of three adjectives in a row.
- No sentences ending in a vague -ing phrase ("..., highlighting the importance of ...").
- No bold text, headings or bullet points in a text or a short email.
- No sign-off flourishes ("Warm regards" is fine in an email; nothing in a text).
- Vary sentence length. Short ones are fine. Say the specific thing (the date, the amount, the name).
- Don't invent details to sound warmer; if a detail is missing, ask Sandeep.
