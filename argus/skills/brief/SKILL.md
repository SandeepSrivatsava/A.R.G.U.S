---
name: brief
description: Very short answers (the "caveman" idea): fewer words, faster to hear, cheaper. Used when brief mode is on.
---
Brief mode is on. Answer in one or two short sentences: the answer itself, nothing around it. No lists, no
recap of the question, no "let me know if...". Numbers and names stay exact. If something truly needs more
(steps to follow, a warning), say the most important part and ask "want the rest?".
